## Why this took a night of red checks

Three CI gates were red on correct images, each a true statement about the wrong thing:

1. `missing /usr/bin/gcc` — asserted on images that never install a compiler;
2. `identity file … missing` — asserted on fixtures that never receive the identity;
3. `welcome header printed 0` — `verify-motd.mjs` hardcoded `Welcome to Ubuntu` while the banner became
   `Welcome to MorpheOS`.

The rule that came out of it: **assert where the promise is made**, and a guard that hardcodes a fact the
product may change must know both names and say which it saw.

## What 1.1 still is not

The kernel answers `Linux` here. Our own kernel answers `MorpheOS`, boots without an initramfs and plays the
boot sound, but it is not in this release — the laptop's `b43` (Wi-Fi) and `i915` (panel) on our kernel are
unproven, and a kernel shipped without its drivers is a release that looks right and boots dark.
