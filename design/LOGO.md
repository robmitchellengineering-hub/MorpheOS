# The MorpheOS mark

*A dream resolving into a machine.*

At the centre, a green **geomesh** — a crystalline wireframe, half mathematics, half living
thing — draws itself into being, edge by edge, out of nothing. It is the **intent engine**: the
place where your words become action. It does not arrive whole; it assembles itself, the way a
thought becomes a decision. It turns slowly to the left, unhurried, because it is building
something that will not fall apart.

Around it, three **rings of light** — pink, cyan, blue — orbit on wandering axes. They are the
**deterministic layer** that guards the core: the rule that *AI proposes, and something
trustworthy decides.* They are the boundary between a suggestion and an action. A glowing **data
bulge** travels each ring — energy moving, life moving — because MorpheOS is never idle; it is
quietly checking on itself.

Behind it all, a **field of stars** — the depth of what is possible. MorpheOS is named for
Morpheus, the god of dreams, and the starfield is the dream-space the machine lives in: every
possibility, held calmly, waiting.

The **wordmark** is honest: *MorpheOS*, in white, so it can always be read. And beneath it, the
promise the whole thing is built around: **"Never breaks · Heals itself."**

The colours carry the meaning:

- **Green** — the colour of healing, of life, of "it fixed itself before you noticed."
- **Black** — the depth the possibilities live in, not the absence of anything.
- **White** — clarity, honesty, a system you can read.

And the animation tells the whole story in a few seconds: the mark **phases through cyberpunk
colours and lands on green** — because MorpheOS is not a machine that flashes and distracts; it
is one that settles, resolves, and holds. Order out of chaos. A dream that became an operating
system.
